The game-exact sim follows the game's own code: belt segments with exact float32 lengths, item spacing, merge priority, splitter round robin, undergrounds, arms, machines, storage and extractors, 60 ticks a second. Open belt starts become inputs. Open belt ends that face out of the build become outputs (red tiles; inputs are green); any other open end stops, as in game. Tap a tile to add or remove an input or output. Labs take every item (not traced yet); power plants take one load of fuel and boost what is in range.
